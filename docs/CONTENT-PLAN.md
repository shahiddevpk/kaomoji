## Quality gate (no thin content)

Every page or post must pass all of these before it ships:

1. 900+ words of the post's own text for a blog post: measure the `<article>` up to "More faces for this occasion", so the related-post cards (about 650 words) are never counted. FAQs and tables count only if they carry real information. Measure density against the same text.
2. Every section answers something a searcher wants, with faces or steps they can use. No filler intros.
3. Keyword variants ("kaomoji sad" / "sad kaomoji" / "sad kaomojis") are folded into one page or post. Never split them into separate near-duplicates.
4. Faces and wording are unique across the site. Reuse a face only when it is the best answer.
5. Primary keyword density 0.45% to 0.95% of visible text, plain English, grammar read through.
6. Links to the owning page, and the owning page gets a link back (the `related` array does this).
7. A term with under about 100 monthly searches gets an FAQ on its owner page unless it can fill a full post on its own value.

## Review checklist before each ship

1. Facts: dates, names, and claims checked against the page or a reliable source.
2. Thin-content gate above.
3. Build passes; links resolve; one H1; title 59 characters or fewer.
4. Rendering: copy cards and tables checked in Chrome at desktop and 390px width.
5. Sitemap includes the URL with the right lastmod.
